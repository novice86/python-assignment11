1. Static vs. interactive visualizations
Static visualizations show data in a fixed form, so the viewer cannot change or explore them. 
Interactive visualizations let users click, filter, zoom, and explore different parts of the data. 
This makes it easier to find patterns and details.

2. Advantages of dashboards
Dashboards are useful for real-time data exploration because they show important information in one place. 
Users can quickly filter the data, track changes, and find problems or trends as they happen. 
This helps with faster decision-making.

3. Plotly in professional settings
Tools like Plotly make data easier to understand and communicate. 
Users can hover over charts, filter information, and explore specific data points. 
This makes presentations and reports more engaging and helps people understand the data better.
